03 Facility

One acre now, built to become five.

Stage 1 is a single acre, but it is laid out as the first module of a five-acre site rather than as a standalone farm. Roads, drainage, incoming power and transformer capacity, water storage, the laboratory and the control architecture are sized ahead of the ponds they will eventually serve.

Underground services and the main pipe corridors are routed so that later modules can be built without excavating the working facility, and the master layout reserves land so that no permanent building ends up standing where a future pond block needs to go.

04 Quality

The paperwork is part of the product.

A buyer of food-grade biomass is buying evidence as much as powder. The facility is designed so that evidence exists by default rather than being reconstructed afterwards.

Traceable from inoculation to pack

Batch identity is carried through pond, harvest, dryer, mill and pack, so a finished unit can be walked back to the culture it came from.

Laboratories on site

Quality-control and microbiology laboratories are part of Stage 1, not a later addition — routine testing happens on the site that made the material.

Water qualified by analysis

Source water is tested for hardness, salinity, metals, nitrate and microbiological load, and treatment is selected from those results. No water source is approved on the strength of how it looks.

Hygienic layout

Product-contact surfaces, drainage falls, personnel routes and the separation of raw material from finished goods are set at design stage, where they are still cheap to get right.

Compliance posture

The design is built to support FSSAI requirements and applicable Indian food, environmental and labour regulation, and to leave export certification and higher-grade production open as later steps.

BlueBloom holds no food-safety certifications yet — the facility is in engineering. Registrations and certifications will be listed here as they are actually granted, and never before.
