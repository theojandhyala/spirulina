### Subject: Quality control and microbiology laboratory equipment — BlueBloom Spirulina Production Facility, Hyderabad Region

We are equipping two laboratories at a spirulina production facility: a quality
control laboratory and a physically separate microbiology laboratory.

## 1. The most important instrument

Daily microscopy of every cultivation pond is the single most valuable process
control in spirulina production. A rotifer population can collapse a pond within
48 to 72 hours, and it is detectable under a microscope days before it is visible
to the eye.

Please give particular attention to the microscope specification. It will be used
every day by production staff, not occasionally by a specialist.

## 6. Tests we expect to outsource — please quote as a service

The following require capability we do not intend to build in house. Please
quote per-sample rates if you offer NABL-accredited testing.

| Parameter | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | ≤ 3.0 mg/kg |
| Cadmium | ≤ 1.0 mg/kg |
| Mercury | ≤ 0.1 mg/kg |
| Arsenic | ≤ 2.0 mg/kg |
| ***E. coli*, *Salmonella*, *S. aureus*** | Absence, confirmatory |
| **Microcystins** | **≤ 1.0 µg/g** |

**Microcystin testing is a release parameter for every batch.** *Arthrospira
platensis* does not produce microcystins, but contaminating cyanobacteria in an
open pond can, and it is the principal recognised safety hazard associated with
commercially sold spirulina. Please state your method, detection limit and
turnaround time for this test specifically.
