### Subject: Spirulina harvesting, dewatering and washing equipment — BlueBloom Spirulina Production Facility, Hyderabad Region

We invite your budgetary quotation for equipment to harvest spirulina biomass
from open raceway ponds and dewater it to a wet cake suitable for drying.

## 1. A note on the organism — please do not quote a centrifuge

Spirulina grows as filaments roughly 100 to 500 µm long. Unlike single-celled
microalgae, it can be separated on a fine screen. **Screen filtration is the
intended method and centrifugation is not required.** Screening is gentler on the
filaments and very much cheaper to run.

If your standard offering for microalgae is a centrifuge, it is probably not
suited to this duty. We would still welcome your comments.

## 2. Design basis

Equipment is to be sized on the peak duty below, which carries deliberate margin
over current expected output.

| Parameter | Peak design value |
| --- | --- |
| Dry biomass harvested | 24 kg/day |
| Culture harvest concentration | 0.65 g/L |
| Required capture efficiency | 92 % minimum |
| **Culture volume harvested** | **40 m³/day** |
| Harvest window | 5 hours |
| **Harvest flow rate** | **8 m³/h duty** |
| Turndown requirement | Stable operation to 30 % of peak |

## 3. Scope of supply

| Item | Requirement |

## 5. Screen requirements

| Parameter | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Aperture | 30 – 50 µm |
| Hydraulic loading | 5 – 15 m³/h per m² of screen |
| Minimum screen area at design flow | 1.5 m² |
| Screen material | Food-grade, corrosion resistant in alkaline service |
| Cleaning | Removable and cleanable **without tools** |
| Inspection | Screen condition inspectable at every harvest |

A torn screen passes biomass to the filtrate, where the loss is invisible until
yields are reconciled. Ease of inspection matters.

## 6. Pump requirements — low shear

The harvest pump handles live biomass. **Filament damage reduces yield and
releases intracellular pigment**, so a low-shear pump type is required. Please
state the pump type you propose and why it is suitable for shear-sensitive
filamentous biomass.

## 7. Washing

| Parameter | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Remove residual culture medium, principally sodium salts |
| Wash water | Treated water, potable standard |
| Indicative wash ratio | 2 L per kg of wet cake |
| Design intent | Meet a finished-product ash specification of ≤ 9 % |

Unwashed biomass produces a product with elevated ash and sodium and a
perceptibly saline taste. Over-washing wastes treated water and begins to leach
soluble intracellular components. Please advise on wash stages and ratio.

## 8. Pressing — a caution

Pressing pressure and duration must be limited to that needed to reach the
target solids. **Over-pressing ruptures filaments and releases phycocyanin into
the press liquor**, visible as a blue-green tinge. This is lost product and lost
value.

Please state how your equipment allows pressure and duration to be controlled
and repeated.

## 9. Service conditions

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Temperature | Ambient, up to 40 °C culture |
| Product contact | All wetted surfaces **food-contact grade** |
| Hygiene | Fully drainable, cleanable, no dead legs or stagnant pockets |
| Location | Enclosed process building |
