## 3. The governing tolerance — please read

**The pond floor level tolerance is ±15 mm across the whole pond.**

This is tighter than general civil work and it is not negotiable. The culture
operates at only 200 mm depth, so a 20 mm level error is a 10 % variation in
culture depth, and a 50 mm error produces shallow zones that overheat and deep
zones that never reach design flow velocity.

| Element | Tolerance |
| --- | --- |
| **Pond floor, level across the whole pond** | **±15 mm** |
| Pond floor, local irregularity under a 3 m straightedge | ±10 mm |
| Wall top level | ±10 mm |
| Baffle wall alignment | ±20 mm over pond length |
| Channel width | ±25 mm |

The floor is to be constructed **level, not to a fall**. Drainage is by a local
sump. The floor will be surveyed on a 5 m grid after construction and again
after lining, and both surveys must be accepted before the pond is filled.

If you cannot achieve ±15 mm, please tell us in your quotation rather than at
site.

## 4. Channel geometry

Pond geometry permanently determines the energy needed to circulate the culture,
which is about one third of this facility's electricity consumption. It cannot
be improved later by operational means.

| Element | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Bend form | Semicircular or near-semicircular, **not square** |
| Flow deflectors at bends | Required |
| Baffle wall ends | **Rounded, not square-ended** |
| Channel cross-section | Uniform, with no local constrictions, steps or projections |
| Wall and floor finish | Smooth, free of local depressions |
| Dead zones | None permitted; verified by dye or float test at commissioning |
| Carbonation sump | Local deepening of the channel floor, 1.0–1.5 m below floor level, on a straight, watertight, fully drainable |

Square-ended baffle walls and undeflected bends cause both excess circulation
power and biomass settlement. A settled deposit at a bend turns anaerobic within
days and becomes a permanent contamination source.

## 6. Lining

| Parameter | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Geomembrane, **food-contact grade** |
| Preferred materials | HDPE or LLDPE, UV-stabilised with carbon black |
| Minimum thickness | 0.75 mm; **1.0 mm preferred** for exposed service |
| Certification | Food-contact certification to be supplied with the goods |
| Service conditions | Permanently exposed, high solar exposure, culture at pH 9.5–10.5 |
| Expected service life | Please state |

Food-contact certification is a firm requirement. The liner is a product-contact
surface for the whole life of the culture. General-purpose canal or pond liner
is not acceptable.

### 6.1 Subgrade, anchorage and testing

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Subgrade | Smooth, rolled, compacted; **maximum 10 mm particle size in the top 150 mm** |
| Geotextile cushion | 200–300 g/m² where subgrade is granular or contains stone |
| Subgrade acceptance | Inspected and accepted in writing before lining begins |
| Anchor trench | Continuous at crest, indicatively 500 mm × 500 mm |
| Penetrations | Minimised, sealed with proprietary boots or flanged details |
| Seaming | Thermal wedge welding; extrusion welding for detail work |
| Trial welds | At the start of each shift and on any change of conditions |
| Non-destructive testing | **100 % of seams**, by air-pressure test of the dual-track weld or vacuum box |
| Destructive testing | Sampled, peel and shear tested |
| Leak test | Pond filled with water and monitored before the culture is introduced |
| Records | Full weld and test records supplied at handover |

### 6.2 Underdrainage and venting

An empty lined pond is subject to uplift from groundwater or trapped gas. Ponds
are emptied for cleaning at the end of the monsoon, when groundwater is highest,
so **the empty pond is the governing case**.

Please include passive vent points through the embankment, and a granular
drainage layer beneath the liner if the investigation shows groundwater may
approach floor level.

### 6.3 Replaceability

The liner has a shorter design life than the pond structure and will be replaced
at least once. Please detail anchorage and penetrations so that replacement does
not require demolition: re-excavatable anchor trenches, demountable penetrations,
and no fixed items over the liner.
