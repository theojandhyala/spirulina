We invite your budgetary quotation for equipment to dry spirulina wet cake and
to mill and sieve it to a food-grade powder.

## 1. The critical requirement — product temperature

Spirulina's commercial value depends substantially on **phycocyanin**, the blue
pigment that gives the product its colour. Phycocyanin is heat labile. Drying is
the process step at which product quality is most easily destroyed.

| Parameter | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| **Maximum product temperature** | **60 °C** |
| Maximum product temperature, brief exposure | 65 °C |
| Target final moisture | 5 % |
| Maximum final moisture | 7 % |

Air inlet temperature may exceed the product temperature limit while free water
is present and evaporative cooling holds the product down. **The control problem
is at the end of the cycle**, when free water is gone, evaporative cooling falls
away and product temperature runs up quickly.

Please tell us how your equipment controls product temperature at the end of the
drying cycle, and whether product temperature is measured directly rather than
inferred from air temperature.

## 3. Dryer options

We are open on drying method and would welcome your recommendation.

| Option | Our assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Spray drying | Standard at large scale; brief thermal exposure; higher capital cost; needs feed at lower solids than our press produces |
| Low-temperature hot-air drying of extruded biomass | Suited to this scale; well established for spirulina |
| Solar-assisted drying with controlled auxiliary heat | Lowest energy cost; needs enclosure for hygiene; monsoon performance to be assessed |
| **Open sun drying** | **Not acceptable** — no temperature control, no contamination control, no traceability |

Drying must be a controlled process with recorded parameters, whatever the
energy source.

Please state what heat source your equipment uses. Electrical drying is
approximately 38 % of this facility's total energy consumption, so we are
actively interested in biomass-fired or solar-assisted alternatives.

## 6. Humidity — a building and equipment requirement

Spirulina powder is hygroscopic. Product that reabsorbs moisture above
specification must be re-dried, which means a second thermal exposure and
further pigment loss.

Milling, sieving and packing are to take place in a humidity-controlled space.
Please state the ambient humidity your equipment requires, and whether you can
supply or advise on humidity control.

## 7. What to include in your quotation

- expected effect of your process on phycocyanin retention, with evidence;
- mill type, achievable particle size distribution and temperature rise;
- cleaning arrangements and changeover time;
- reference installations for spirulina or similar heat-sensitive products;
