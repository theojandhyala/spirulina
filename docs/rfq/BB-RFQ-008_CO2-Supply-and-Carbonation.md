Dear Sir or Madam,

### Subject: Food-grade carbon dioxide supply and carbonation system — BlueBloom Spirulina Production Facility, Hyderabad Region

Carbon dioxide is the carbon source for spirulina growth at this facility. We
invite your quotation for gas supply, the injection system, or both.

## 1. Demand

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Carbon content of dry biomass | 46 % |
| Peak carbon demand | 11.0 kg C/day |
| Stoichiometric CO₂ demand | 40.5 kg/day |
| **Design utilisation efficiency** | **65 % minimum** |
| Delivered CO₂ requirement, peak | 62 kg/day |
| **Design basis for supply and storage** | **70 kg/day** |
| Annual consumption, indicative | ≈ 13 tonnes |
| Consumption pattern | Daylight hours only; no night-time demand |

## 2. Gas quality — food grade required

The CO₂ dissolves into a culture that becomes a food product. **Food or beverage
grade CO₂ is required.** Please state the grade you supply, the specification it
meets, and confirm that a certificate of analysis accompanies each delivery.

Industrial-grade CO₂ is not acceptable.

## 3. Supply arrangement

Please quote for whichever of the following you offer:

| Option | Please state |
| --- | --- |
| Bulk liquid CO₂ with vessel on hire | Vessel size, hire charge, refill price, minimum offtake |
| Cylinder supply | Cylinder size, price, exchange arrangement, delivery frequency |

At 70 kg/day peak our offtake is modest, so please be clear about any minimum
quantity or standing charge.

Delivery is to a site in the Hyderabad region. Please confirm you serve this
area and state normal delivery lead time, including during monsoon.

## 4. Injection system

| Parameter | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Contacting method | **Carbonation sump or equivalent contacting device** |
| Design utilisation | ≥ 65 % |
| Surface sparging | **Not acceptable as the primary method** — utilisation is typically only 30–40 % |
| Control | pH-controlled, on the pond pH signal |
| Injection timing | Daylight hours, matched to photosynthetic demand |
| Ponds served | 4 production ponds, 435 m² each |

We are constructing a carbonation sump as a civil structure, indicatively 1.0 to
1.5 m deep in the channel floor. Contactor depth is the principal determinant of
utilisation efficiency.

**Please advise what sump depth your equipment needs to achieve 65 % utilisation.**
This must be settled before the pond floor is built, because it cannot be changed
afterwards without breaking out and reconstructing the pond.

## 5. Safety

CO₂ is heavier than air and accumulates in low-lying and confined spaces.

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Storage location | Outdoors, ventilated, away from excavations, sumps and building entries |
| Fixed CO₂ detection | In any enclosed space with CO₂ piping or storage |
| Carbonation sumps | Treated as confined spaces for entry |
| Relief and venting | Per applicable standards |

Please include detection equipment in your quotation, or state clearly that it
is excluded.

## 6. What to include in your quotation

In addition to **BB-RFQ-000, Instructions to Suppliers**, attached:

- gas grade and specification, with confirmation of certificate of analysis per delivery;
- supply arrangement, pricing and any minimum offtake or standing charge;
- injection equipment proposed, and the utilisation efficiency you will stand behind;
- **required sump depth to achieve 65 % utilisation**;
- pH control equipment, or interface requirements if we supply it;
- CO₂ detection and safety equipment;
- delivery lead time and monsoon-season reliability.
