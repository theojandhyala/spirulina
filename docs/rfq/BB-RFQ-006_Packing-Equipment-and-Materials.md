### Subject: Packing equipment and packaging materials for spirulina powder — BlueBloom Spirulina Production Facility, Hyderabad Region

We invite your budgetary quotation for packing equipment, packaging materials,
or both. Please state which you are quoting for.

## 1. Product characteristics

Dried spirulina powder is **hygroscopic, oxygen sensitive and light sensitive**.
Phycocyanin, the pigment that carries much of the product's value, degrades on
exposure to light and oxygen. The powder cakes on moisture pickup.

Packaging is not merely a container for this product. It is what preserves the
quality that the drying process was designed to protect.

## 2. Volumes

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Peak product to pack | 25.3 kg/day |
| Annual product, Stage 1 | ≈ 4,130 kg |
| Expansion | Facility is designed to expand to five acres, roughly six times this volume |

At these volumes **throughput is not our constraint — changeover time between
pack formats is**. Please bear this in mind when proposing equipment.

## 3. Pack formats required

| Format | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Bulk food-grade | Food-grade liner within an outer carton or drum |
| Retail powder | Laminated barrier pouch |
| Tablets | Barrier container or blister, at a later stage |

Please quote for bulk and retail formats. Tablet packing may be enquired
separately later.

## 4. Barrier requirements

| Barrier | Bulk | Retail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moisture | Required | Required |
| Oxygen | Required | Required |
| Light | Required | Required |
| Inert gas flush | Please quote as an option | Please quote as an option |

Please state the oxygen and moisture transmission rates of any laminate you
propose, and the basis on which you would expect it to hold product quality.

## 5. Compliance

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Food contact | All materials suitable for food contact, with documentation |
| Labelling | To comply with FSSAI and Legal Metrology requirements |
| **Batch coding** | **Every pack**, traceable to the production batch |
| Retail declarations | Provision for nutritional and ingredient declarations |

Batch coding on every pack is a firm requirement. Our traceability system must
link a pack back to the pond the biomass came from, and forward from a pond to
every pack containing its biomass.

## 6. Shelf life

We will establish shelf life by a stability study on our own product in the
selected packaging. We are not able to adopt a shelf life from a supplier's
literature.

If you can support a stability study, please quote for it separately.

## 7. Environment

Packing takes place in a humidity-controlled area. Please state any ambient
conditions your equipment requires.

## 8. What to include in your quotation

In addition to **BB-RFQ-000, Instructions to Suppliers**, attached:

- equipment proposed, with capacity and changeover time between formats;
- laminate structure with barrier properties stated;
- minimum order quantities for packaging materials;
- artwork and tooling costs, and lead time;
- coding equipment;
- food-contact documentation;
- delivery period.
