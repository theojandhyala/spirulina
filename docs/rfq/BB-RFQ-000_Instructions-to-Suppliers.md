## 1. About this enquiry series

BlueBloom Spirulina Private Limited is developing a commercial spirulina
cultivation, harvesting, drying and packaging facility in the Hyderabad region
of Telangana. The first stage occupies one acre and is laid out as the first
module of a five-acre development.

This document sets out the terms that apply to every enquiry in the BB-RFQ
series. It is issued together with a package enquiry letter which contains the
scope and technical requirements specific to your supply.

Please read both documents before quoting.

## 2. Status of each enquiry — please read

Each enquiry letter is marked as either **firm** or **budgetary**. The
distinction matters and is stated honestly:

| Marking | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Firm enquiry** | The scope is settled. We intend to place an order against the successful quotation. |
| **Budgetary enquiry** | The facility is in engineering. Quantities and duties are a preliminary design basis and may change once site investigation and cultivation trials are complete. We are seeking budget pricing and technical guidance, not committing to purchase. |

We would rather tell you this at the outset than have you price a firm order
that does not follow. Where a budgetary enquiry later becomes firm, we will
re-issue it and give you the opportunity to confirm or revise your price.

## 4. Food-grade requirements

This facility produces a food product. The following applies to every enquiry
in this series where the item touches the product, the culture, or the water
that will become the culture:

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Product-contact materials | Suitable for food contact, with documentation |
| Certification | Certificates of conformity to be supplied with the goods, not on request afterwards |
| Alkaline service | Product-contact items operate in an alkaline culture at pH 9.5 to 10.5 |
| Cleanability | Surfaces smooth, drainable, and accessible for cleaning |
| Reagents and consumables | Food grade, with a certificate of analysis per lot including heavy-metal content |

Heavy metals concentrate in the biomass and are subject to limits in the
finished product. A reagent or component that is acceptable in general industrial
use may not be acceptable here. If you are unsure whether your standard offering
meets this, please ask us rather than assume.

## 5. Standards

Design, manufacture and installation shall comply with the latest applicable
editions of Indian standards. Where you propose an international standard in
place of an Indian one, please say so and state which.

Please quote the exact standard and revision you have worked to.

## 8. Evaluation

Quotations will be assessed on whole-life cost, not purchase price alone. The
following carry weight:

- suitability for the duty stated in the package enquiry;
- energy consumption in operation;
- availability of spare parts and service support in India;
- expected service life;
- ease of maintenance and cleaning;
- quality of the technical response;
- delivery period;
- price.

A quotation that is cheapest to buy and most expensive to run will not win.

## 9. Confidentiality

preparing a quotation, and should not be passed to third parties without our
agreement.

## 10. No obligation

an order, and we are not bound to accept the lowest or any quotation.
