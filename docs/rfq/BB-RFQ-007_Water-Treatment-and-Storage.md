## 5. What matters in this application

Water quality drives more than the treatment plant. The parameters below matter
because of what they do downstream:

| Parameter | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Hardness, calcium, magnesium** | Precipitate as carbonates at culture pH 9.5–10.5, consuming alkalinity. The governing case is accumulation over successive evaporation cycles, not concentration in a single batch. |
| **Total dissolved solids** | Accumulates in the culture with every evaporation cycle, forcing a higher blowdown rate. Saline source water therefore raises water consumption and effluent volume across the whole facility. |
| **Heavy metals** | Concentrate in the biomass and are limited in the finished food product. |
| **Fluoride** | A recognised regional groundwater concern in Telangana. |
| **Residual chlorine** | Harmful to the culture; any chlorination must be followed by dechlorination. |
