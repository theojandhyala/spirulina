## 2. Duty

| Parameter | Value |

Please state the power your unit actually requires to achieve 0.25 m/s in the
channel described, and how you have determined it.

## 3. Energy — this is the main selection criterion

Pond circulation is approximately **one third of this facility's total energy
consumption**, and it runs continuously. Over a ten-year life, the difference in
running cost between an efficient unit and an inefficient one will exceed the
purchase price of either.

We will assess quotations on power consumption at the stated duty as much as on
purchase price. Please give us a figure you are prepared to stand behind, and
say whether you will demonstrate it at commissioning.

| Parameter | Value |

| Culture temperature | Up to 40 °C |
| Maximum ambient temperature | 45 °C |
| Equipment surface temperature in sun | Up to 65 °C |
| Exposure | Fully outdoor, monsoon rain, high solar, dusty |
| Duty | Continuous |

## 5. Design requirements

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Product contact | All wetted surfaces **food-contact grade** and suitable for the alkaline service above |
| Corrosion resistance | Suitable for continuous alkaline immersion and splash |
| Blade clearance to pond floor | 20 – 30 mm |
| Blade clearance to walls | 20 – 30 mm |
| Bearings | Selected for continuous duty; not lubricated into the culture |
| Shaft seals | Suitable for alkaline, abrasive service |
| Gearbox | Rated for continuous duty at the stated ambient |
| Drive | Suitable for variable-frequency drive operation |
| Backup power | Unit to be connectable to backup supply |
| **Minimum design life** | **10 years** |

Bearing arrangement, gearbox rating and shaft-seal design are the items that
determine whether these units survive. Loss of circulation risks loss of the
entire culture in a pond, so reliability is valued above first cost.

## 6. Maintenance and safety

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Safe access for inspection, lubrication and cleaning |
| Removal | Motor and gearbox removable without draining the pond, if achievable |
| Guarding | Full guarding of all rotating parts, couplings and shafts |
| Emergency stop | Reachable from the pond edge where an operator normally stands, not only at the drive |
| Lubricants | Food-grade where any possibility of product contact exists |

## 7. What to include in your quotation

In addition to **BB-RFQ-000, Instructions to Suppliers**, attached:

- power consumption at 0.25 m/s for the pond described, and your basis;
- materials of construction for all wetted parts, with food-contact documentation;
- bearing, seal and gearbox selection, with expected service intervals;
- whether you will guarantee the velocity and the power figure at commissioning;
- reference installations in India, preferably in algae or aquaculture;
- recommended spares for two years;
- delivery period.
