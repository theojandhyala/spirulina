### Subject: Annual supply of culture nutrients and chemicals — BlueBloom Spirulina Production Facility, Hyderabad Region

We invite your quotation for the annual supply of nutrient reagents for

## 1. Food-grade requirement — please read before quoting

These reagents dissolve into a culture that becomes a **food product**. Heavy
metals present in a reagent concentrate in the biomass and are subject to limits
in the finished product.

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Grade | **Food grade** |
| Certificate of analysis | Per lot, supplied with the goods |
| **Heavy metal content** | **Must be stated on the certificate of analysis** — lead, cadmium, mercury, arsenic |
| Fertiliser or technical grade | **Not acceptable** without documented heavy-metal analysis and our written approval |

Please do not quote fertiliser-grade material against these items on price
grounds without telling us plainly that is what you are offering.

## 3. Nitrogen source — we would welcome your view

We have not yet fixed our nitrogen source, and the decision will be made on
cultivation trial results rather than on price alone.

| Source | Consideration |
| --- | --- |
| **Sodium nitrate** | Stable, predictable, directly available to the culture. Higher cost, higher sodium load on the medium. |
| **Urea** | Markedly cheaper per kg of nitrogen, lower sodium. But hydrolyses to ammonia, which is toxic to the culture at our operating pH and is lost to atmosphere. |

Please quote both. If you have experience supplying either into algae
cultivation, we would value your comment.

## 4. Micronutrient formulation

Our reference formulation is the A5 micronutrient solution used with Zarrouk's
medium. If you supply a pre-blended micronutrient mix for algae culture, please
quote it and state the composition.
