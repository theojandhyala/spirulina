## 3. Hygienic finishes — the defining requirement

This is a food production facility. The following are not optional finishes;
they are what makes the building fit for purpose.

| Element | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Floor, wet process areas | Impervious, non-slip, chemical resistant — epoxy or polyurethane screed |
| Floor falls | 1 in 80 to 1 in 100 to drainage in wet areas |
| **Wall and floor junctions** | **Coved, minimum 25 mm radius** |
| Wall finish | Smooth, impervious, washable, light coloured, to minimum 2.1 m |
| Drainage direction | **From clean areas towards dirty areas, never the reverse** |
| Floor drainage | Trapped gullies with removable gratings |
| Ceilings in product areas | Sealed, cleanable, no accessible void |
| **Timber** | **Not permitted in product areas** |
| Glass in product areas | Minimised; laminated or shatter-protected where required |
| Lighting over product | Shatter-protected fittings |
| Ventilation | Positive pressure in packing areas relative to adjacent areas |
| Humidity control | Required in drying, milling and packing areas |

## 4. Pest exclusion

Open cultivation ponds attract birds and insects. Physical exclusion from the
buildings is the practical control, and it depends on details fixed now.

| Element | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| External doors | Close-fitting, self-closing, with brush or blade seals |
| Openings and vents | Screened |
| Windows in product areas | Sealed, or screened where openable |
| Service penetrations | Sealed |
| Drainage | Trapped and screened against rodent entry |
| External surrounds | Clear strip maintained around buildings |

## 5. Drainage — three separate systems

| System | Content | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stormwater | Clean runoff from roofs, roads, open areas | Site discharge |
| Process effluent | Blowdown, wash water, cleaning water | Effluent management |
| Foul | Personnel facilities | Septic or municipal |

**These three systems must remain physically separate throughout, with no
cross-connection provided under any circumstance, including for emergency
relief.** A single cross-connection turns a contained process discharge into an
uncontrolled environmental release.

## 8. Expansion constraint

The site is the first acre of a five-acre development. **No permanent building
may be sited where a future pond block is planned.** We will provide the master
layout showing reserved areas. Please confirm you will work to it.
